# Scripting: Story Architect

## Configurable Duration Tiers
| Tier | Duration | Spoken Word Budget | Ideal Shot Count |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`short`** | `20–30 s` | `55–80 words` | `8–11 shots` |
| **`standard`** *(default)* | `35–45 s` | `90–125 words` | `12–16 shots` |
| **`extended`** | `45–60 s` | `125–175 words` | `15–20 shots` |

## Five-Beat Narrative Spine
1. **Hook (0–3 s):** Immediately establish the bizarre premise or urgent question.
2. **Context (3–8 s):** Supply *only* the minimum background needed for someone with zero prior knowledge.
3. **Escalation (8–28 s):** Each line introduces a fresh obstacle, complication, or mechanism—strict chronological causality, zero filler.
4. **Payoff (28–36 s):** Deliver the core revelation, clever twist, or climax clearly.
5. **Ending (final 3–5 s):** A sharp punchline, memorable reflection, or **optional natural loop** (only use looping when the final phrase connects effortlessly back to line 1 without sounding forced).
