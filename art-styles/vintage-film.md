# Art Style 11: Vintage Film (`vintage-film`)

**Runtime ID:** `"vintage-film"` (`art_style.js`)

## Visual Specification
- **Colour Palette:** Warm 16mm Kodachrome / archival sepia-tinted palette — muted warm highlights `#F2DEC0`, deep brownish-charcoal blacks `#1E1714`, dusty crimson `#A63A32`, olive `#6B7448`, faded teal `#4E7478`.
- **Lighting:** Soft warm tungsten or hazy afternoon natural falloff with heavy organic dark corner vignette.
- **Character Design:** Period-appropriate silhouettes and clothing tones; classic documentary restraint.
- **Environment Design:** Softened contrast, aged archival paper/film warmth, subtle film gate borders and organic lens softness.
- **Camera Language:** Slow documentary push-ins, gentle handheld micro-weave (`±2 px`), contemplative 3–5 s pacing.
- **Texture & Materials:** Warm sepia/Kodachrome color shift (`sepia(0.32) contrast(1.08) saturate(0.82)`), tiled animated **16mm film grain**, subtle vertical scratch/dust specks, rounded corner film vignette.
- **Animation Characteristics:** 24 fps cinema cadence, subtle gate flicker (`sin(t * 24) * 0.02`), measured motion.
- **Scene Transitions:** Smooth film fades, warm projector burn/dissolve transitions.
- **Typography & Subtitle Compatibility:** Classic editorial serif (`Caladea`) or typewriter (`FreeMono`) cream `#F5E9D3` captions with dark umber shadow and faded crimson `#D94E41` keyword highlight, `y = 1400`.
- **Negative Prompts & Unwanted Artifacts:** Avoid hyper-saturated neon RGB colors, glossy modern vector pill bubbles, or distracting heavy scratches over faces.
