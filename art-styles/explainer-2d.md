# Art Style 10: Explainer 2D (`explainer-2d`)

**Runtime ID:** `"explainer-2d"` (`art_style.js`)

## Visual Specification
- **Colour Palette:** High-clarity educational infographic palette — clean studio warm white `#F8F7F2`, slate navy `#1E293B`, cobalt `#2563EB`, coral red `#E11D48`, emerald `#059669`, amber `#F59E0B`.
- **Lighting:** Bright even studio clarity; zero dark vignetting or muddy shadows.
- **Character Design:** Approachable clean geometric heroes paired with unambiguous medical/technical callouts and leader lines.
- **Environment Design:** Subtle editorial blueprint/dot grid, clean rounded info cards, crisp directional arrows, color-coded cutaways.
- **Camera Language:** Smooth analytical zooms (`0.06–0.22`), steady pans between diagram nodes, clear focal hierarchy.
- **Texture & Materials:** High-legibility subtle blueprint dot-grid overlay (`alpha 0.08`), clean contrast (`1.06`), crisp vector sharp rendering.
- **Animation Characteristics:** Smooth 30 fps educational motion, synchronized label pops (`eob`), clean data bars/progress counters.
- **Scene Transitions:** Crisp card slides, light flash cuts.
- **Typography & Subtitle Compatibility:** Ultra-legible `Inter Black` / `Inter ExtraBold` inside a sleek translucent dark pill or heavy outline, amber `#F59E0B` / `#FFD43B` key term highlight, `y = 1390`.
- **Negative Prompts & Unwanted Artifacts:** Avoid messy horror textures, heavy retro pixelation, low-contrast pastel text on light backgrounds, or colliding annotation pills.
