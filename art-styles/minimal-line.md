# Art Style 07: Minimal Line (`minimal-line`)

**Runtime ID:** `"minimal-line"` (`art_style.js`)

## Visual Specification
- **Colour Palette:** Editorial high-key off-white sketchbook paper `#F7F4EB`, crisp charcoal ink `#1C1D21`, single punchy accent hue (vermilion `#D9383A` or cobalt `#2D62B5`).
- **Lighting:** Bright uniform paper illumination with zero dark heavy shading.
- **Character Design:** Clean expressive ink-line silhouettes, strong negative space around figures, unmistakable gesture lines.
- **Environment Design:** Sparse architectural outlines and clean minimal props; 60%+ clean breathing space around the focal subject.
- **Camera Language:** Calm, deliberate pans and centered tracking shots that give lines room to breathe.
- **Texture & Materials:** High-key luminance lift + subtle crisp ink edge enhancement + warm archival sketchbook paper tint (`multiply` alpha `0.06`).
- **Animation Characteristics:** Fluid line movement, subtle 15 fps organic wobble option, understated crisp reactions.
- **Scene Transitions:** Clean paper-white fades, minimal slide cuts.
- **Typography & Subtitle Compatibility:** Clean serif (`Caladea`) or crisp charcoal `Inter`, vermilion `#D9383A` key word highlight on light background, `y = 1380`.
- **Negative Prompts & Unwanted Artifacts:** Avoid cluttered busy backgrounds, neon glows, harsh dark vignettes, or overly saturated rainbow palettes.
