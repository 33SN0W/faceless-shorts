# Art Style 13: Watercolour (`watercolour`)

**Runtime ID:** `"watercolour"` (`art_style.js`)

## Visual Specification
- **Colour Palette:** Painterly soft mineral wash — warm cold-press cotton paper `#F6EFE2`, burnt sienna `#B85A3F`, ultramarine wash `#3E689C`, ochre gold `#D9A74A`, sage wash `#6E9C7A`, charcoal ink `#28242C`.
- **Lighting:** Soft diffused daylight wash; paper brightness provides natural highlights.
- **Character Design:** Gentle painterly brushwork silhouettes with delicate ink accents and soft warm edge bleed.
- **Environment Design:** Textured cold-press paper background, translucent layered color washes, soft atmospheric horizon fades.
- **Camera Language:** Graceful gliding pans and gentle floating drifts; calm storybook pacing.
- **Texture & Materials:** Multiplied **cold-press watercolor paper grain** texture (`alpha 0.22`) + soft pigment bleed blur layer (`blur(3px)` blended via `soft-light`) + warm slightly desaturated painterly wash (`saturate(0.92) brightness(1.04)`).
- **Animation Characteristics:** Smooth gentle eases (`eio`), soft page/ink fade-ins.
- **Scene Transitions:** Soft cross-dissolve washes, warm paper fades.
- **Typography & Subtitle Compatibility:** Classic serif (`Caladea`) on clean paper strip or crisp dark umber outline, crimson `#B83630` keyword highlight, `y = 1390`.
- **Negative Prompts & Unwanted Artifacts:** Avoid harsh digital neon glows, heavy pixel blocks, rigid flat vector boxes, or crushed pure-black digital shadows.
