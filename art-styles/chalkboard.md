# Art Style 02: Chalkboard (`chalkboard`)

**Runtime ID:** `"chalkboard"` (`art_style.js`)

## Visual Specification
- **Colour Palette:** Deep matte blackboard slate `#18221D` / `#1D2823`, primary chalk white `#F3F0E6`, chalk pastel accents: canary `#F6D365`, sky cyan `#7CD4F2`, coral pink `#F28C8C`, mint `#8CE0A8`.
- **Lighting:** Soft overhead classroom ambient vignette; luminous bright chalk edges against shadowed slate corners.
- **Character Design:** Expressive sketchy outline silhouettes and bright pastel chalk fills; faces stand out cleanly as bright white chalk ovals against dark slate.
- **Environment Design:** Matte slate surface with subtle erased chalk dust smudges, hand-drawn callout arrows, dashed chalk diagrams, and underlines.
- **Camera Language:** Smooth lateral slides across a wide virtual chalkboard + crisp zoom-ins onto diagrams and character reactions.
- **Texture & Materials:** Procedural slate grain + high-pass chalk contour extraction + chalk-dust stipple overlay blended cleanly over dark slate.
- **Animation Characteristics:** Hand-drawn progressive stroke reveals, slight organic chalk jitter (`12 fps` subtle hand-drawn feel), bouncy diagram pop-ins.
- **Scene Transitions:** Chalkboard swipe/wipe erase transitions or quick clean slate cuts.
- **Typography & Subtitle Compatibility:** Crisp hand-styled or clean `Inter Black` / `FreeMono` white chalk text with dark slate border (`#111814`), chalk-yellow `#F6D365` keyword highlight, `y = 1360`.
- **Negative Prompts & Unwanted Artifacts:** Avoid glossy 3D plastic specular highlights, harsh neon RGB glow, pure `#000000` digital voids without slate texture, or dark-on-dark unreadable text.
