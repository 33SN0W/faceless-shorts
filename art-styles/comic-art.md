# Art Style 05: Comic Art (`comic-art`)

**Runtime ID:** `"comic-art"` (`art_style.js`)

## Visual Specification
- **Colour Palette:** Punchy CMYK graphic-novel palette — hero crimson `#E62E3D`, cobalt `#2B6CB0`, canary yellow `#FFD12E`, deep India ink `#121118`, crisp newsprint `#FAF5E8`.
- **Lighting:** High-contrast dramatic graphic lighting with crisp cel shadows and bright specular pops.
- **Character Design:** Bold black India ink contours, exaggerated comic reactions, expressive brows, action speed lines, and punchy pose silhouettes.
- **Environment Design:** Stylized graphic backdrops anchored with subtle Ben-Day halftone shading and bold ink frame borders.
- **Camera Language:** Dynamic Dutch-angle reveals (subtle), punchy crash-zooms on comedic/dramatic beats, clear close-ups for facial reaction panels.
- **Texture & Materials:** Contrast boost (`1.18`), saturation boost (`1.22`), pre-rendered diagonal Ben-Day halftone dot screen blended in `multiply` (`alpha 0.14`), plus crisp black comic panel border.
- **Animation Characteristics:** Snappy pop-in overshoots (`eob`), comic impact freeze-frames/stamps, bouncy action smears.
- **Scene Transitions:** Crisp graphic flash cuts, comic panel wipe/snap transitions.
- **Typography & Subtitle Compatibility:** Uppercase `Inter Black` comic caption styling with thick ink stroke, bright canary yellow `#FFD12E` highlighted keyword, `y = 1350`.
- **Negative Prompts & Unwanted Artifacts:** Avoid muddy desaturated washes, soft blurry shadows, noisy photographic grain, or tiny unreadable serif fonts.
