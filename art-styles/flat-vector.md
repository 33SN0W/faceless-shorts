# Art Style 06: Flat Vector (`flat-vector`)

**Runtime ID:** `"flat-vector"` (`art_style.js`)

## Visual Specification
- **Colour Palette:** Clean editorial modern vector palette — coral `#FF6B6B`, teal `#2EC4B6`, royal indigo `#3D5A80`, sunshine `#FFD166`, warm cream `#F7F5EE`, charcoal ink `#1D2026`.
- **Lighting:** Uniform clean ambient illumination; depth created strictly through flat tonal color steps rather than blurred shadows or gradients.
- **Character Design:** Clean geometric proportions, crisp uniform stroke weights, cheerful uncluttered facial clarity.
- **Environment Design:** Minimal architectural silhouettes, clean rounded cards, bold uncluttered backgrounds with strong foreground/background separation.
- **Camera Language:** Smooth eased slides (`eio`), crisp punch-ins (`eo`), clean centered editorial framing.
- **Texture & Materials:** Pure crisp anti-aliased vector fills, contrast/saturation lift (`sat 1.20, contrast 1.08`), zero film grain or grunge noise.
- **Animation Characteristics:** Silky 30 fps geometric spring eases (`eob`), smooth icon scaling, synchronized motion curves.
- **Scene Transitions:** Clean color wipes, subtle white flash cuts, smooth pan transitions.
- **Typography & Subtitle Compatibility:** Bold modern geometric `Inter Black`, clean dark outline + optional pill highlight, `#FFD166` keyword pop, `y = 1380`.
- **Negative Prompts & Unwanted Artifacts:** Avoid heavy noise grain, smudged watercolor textures, dark grungy vignettes, or photorealistic shadows.
