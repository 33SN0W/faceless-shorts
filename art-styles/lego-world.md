# Art Style 09: LEGO World (`lego-world`)

**Runtime ID:** `"lego-world"` (`art_style.js`)

## Visual Specification
- **Colour Palette:** Glossy molded ABS brick primaries — bright brick red `#D91E18`, LEGO yellow `#FFD500`, bright cobalt `#0055BF`, emerald `#00852B`, pure crisp white `#F4F4F4`, jet black `#1B2A34`.
- **Lighting:** Bright miniature tabletop studio lighting with crisp glossy specular highlights and soft tabletop shadows.
- **Character Design:** Blocky miniature proportions, high-contrast saturated uniforms, joyful simple facial expressions.
- **Environment Design:** Toy-scale brick-built buildings, miniature trees, studded ground plates, clean playful miniature proportions.
- **Camera Language:** Miniature macro angles, tabletop tilt-shift feel (soft top/bottom blur), lively stop-motion-inspired punch cuts.
- **Texture & Materials:** Pre-rendered interlocking 12×12 **brick-stud specular grid overlay** + subtle brick-cell quantization (`108×192` macro brick feel blended with crisp detail) + glossy plastic saturation (`sat 1.28, contrast 1.12`).
- **Animation Characteristics:** Crisp 15 fps playful stop-motion cadence option with snappy brick-snap bounces (`eob`).
- **Scene Transitions:** Snappy brick-pop cuts, quick slide transitions.
- **Typography & Subtitle Compatibility:** Chunky rounded bold `Inter Black`, brick-yellow `#FFD500` highlighted keyword, thick dark ink stroke `#1B2A34`, `y = 1360`.
- **Negative Prompts & Unwanted Artifacts:** Avoid grungy film dirt, desaturated washed colors, horror dark fog, or muddy blurry textures without plastic crispness.
