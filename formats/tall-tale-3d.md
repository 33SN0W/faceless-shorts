# Format 1: Tall Tale 3D (`tall-tale`)

**Role in 33SN0W:** Flagship comedic true-story storytelling format.

## Use Cases
- Funny true stories
- Historical scams & confidence tricks
- Crazy legal/financial loopholes
- Audacious heists & bizarre business stories
- Unbelievable historical incidents

## Visual Direction
- **Spatial Model:** Expressive 2D cartoon cut-out characters (`Toon` + custom animal/object cutouts) placed inside saturated 3D environments (`mainStreet`, `interior`, `meadow`).
- **Miniature-World Aesthetics:** Depth-of-field bokeh, sunlit outdoor boardwalks, warm wallpapered rooms, painted plank/brick/felt textures.
- **Physical Comedy:** Faces drive emotion on every beat (`smug`, `shock`, `laugh`, `worried + sweat`, `sly`, `dizzy`). Exaggerated hand-held props (`PROPS.bill`, `stack`, `rubberStamp`, `key`).
- **Camera Language:** Continuous camera motion (push-ins, whip-pans, crane-ups, punch-ins on reveals). Keep subjects inside `x = ±0.45` in two-shots and badges anchored at `y = 290..360` so faces are never occluded.

## Narrative Pacing & Audio
- **Duration:** Standard `35–45 s` (or `45–60 s` for multi-act scams).
- **Shape:** Immediate surprising hook (0–2s) → `"See, ..."` rewind context → escalating steps → clever loophole/trick → comedic twist/punchline.
- **Default Voice & Music:** `am_michael` (`1.05x`), `"bounce"` bed + `pop`, `boing`, `stamp`, `ding`, `whoosh`.

## Art Style Compatibility
- Works cleanly with **all 13 art styles**, especially: `comic-art`, `claymation`, `lego-world`, `ps2-retro`, `vintage-film`, `cgi-motion`, `pixel-art`, `watercolour`.
