# Format 6: Paper Atlas (`paper-atlas`)

**Role in 33SN0W:** Grand historical, geographical, and civilization storytelling in cut paper.

## Use Cases
- Ancient civilizations & forgotten cities
- Historic battles, sieges, and turning points (non-graphic)
- Rise and fall of empires & trade routes
- Legendary voyages, expeditions & geopolitical treaties

## Visual Direction
- **Cut-Paper Craft Aesthetic:** Every element (`K.cut(path, fill)`) has a crisp light-caught paper edge and a soft drop shadow resting on aged parchment (`K.parchment`).
- **Color Discipline:** Crimson reserved for routes, targets, fire, and key highlighted words; gold reserved for era ribbons (`K.ribbon`), crowns, and wax seals (`K.seal`); navy wash (`nightPage`) for night sequences.
- **Geographic Clarity:** Maps open the spatial context first ("where → who → what"), using animated dashed crimson routes (`K.dashedRoute`), miniature paper ships/harbors, and folded timeline strips (`K.timeline`).
- **Non-Violent Historical Representation:** Depict conflicts via routes, maps, buildings, smoke, and outcomes—never graphic violence.

## Narrative Pacing & Audio
- **Duration:** `35–55 s`.
- **Default Voice & Music:** `bm_george` (`1.0x`), `"tension"` (disasters/wars) or `"bed"` (exploration/culture) + `slide`, `whoosh`, `sparkle`, `thud`.

## Art Style Compatibility
- Best with: `watercolour`, `vintage-film`, `comic-art`, `claymation`, `minimal-line`, `explainer-2d`, `pixel-art`.
