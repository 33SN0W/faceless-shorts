# Format 3: Inside Out (`inside-out`)

**Role in 33SN0W:** High-clarity 2D vector science, psychology, and health explainers.

## Use Cases
- Science explainers & everyday phenomena
- Health education & nutrition myths
- Cognitive biases & human psychology
- Mechanical & biological cutaways

## Visual Direction
- **Clean 2D Vector Composition:** Flat geometric shapes, crisp shading layers (base + darker shade + specular highlight), zero muddy gradients unless overridden by an Art Style preset.
- **Visual Language:** Minimal round-head hero, clear color coding (x-ray cyan for internals, danger red for myth-busting X / warnings, yellow for key focus & acid/energy), animated leader tags (`K.tag`), pills (`K.pill`), and timers (`K.stopwatch`).
- **Transitions:** Dynamic punch-ins, slide transitions, myth-buster `redX` slams. Keep hero and graphics within `y = 250..1240` above the caption lane.

## Narrative Pacing & Audio
- **Duration:** `25–45 s`.
- **Shape:** Hook question → x-ray/cutaway view → myth busted → scientific mechanism → time lapse → surprising twist/takeaway.
- **Default Voice & Music:** `am_michael` or `af_heart` (`1.0x`), `"bed"` + `whoosh`, `pop`, `gulp`, `sizzle`, `slam`.

## Art Style Compatibility
- Pairs extraordinarily well with: `flat-vector`, `explainer-2d`, `chalkboard`, `glow-sketch`, `minimal-line`, `comic-art`, `pixel-art`, `watercolour`.
